About Us

More than a community.

CreateJoy is a growing business and community ecosystem focused on people development, business opportunities, and meaningful experiences.

We create an environment where people can develop practical skills, expand their network, explore opportunities, and grow alongside a supportive community.

Our Ecosystem

People and business
growing together.

CreateJoy brings together professional development, business, community, and lifestyle in one environment.

People Development

Join learning sessions, peer mentoring, and hands-on activities to practice communication, leadership, networking, and business skills.

Business Development

Help build partnerships, follow up on opportunities, and support client relationships while learning how business development works in practice.

Projects & Experiences

Coordinate timelines, people, and details for projects and events, from planning through delivery and follow-up.

Community & Lifestyle

Help host gatherings and shared-interest activities that bring people together, from community meetups to lifestyle experiences.

Business Opportunities

Build your own opportunity.

Explore practical roles, contribute to real projects, and develop professionally as part of a growing business and community ecosystem.

HR & People Development

Help welcome and onboard members, coordinate team communication, and support learning activities. A good fit for people who enjoy organizing, listening, and helping others develop.

Project Management

Turn plans into timelines, coordinate owners and logistics, track progress, and close the loop after delivery. A good fit for organized communicators who enjoy solving practical problems.

Business Development

Research potential partners, help manage follow-ups, and support relationship-building. A good fit for curious, proactive people who enjoy connecting with others.

Sales & Client Relationships

Understand client needs, prepare clear follow-ups, and maintain reliable partner communication. Activities and compensation depend on the specific role or opportunity.
